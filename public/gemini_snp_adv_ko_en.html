셋째, 장기적인 투자 안목 유지

During volatile periods, it is crucial to review allocations to defensive sectors such as high-dividend stocks or consumer staples, and to secure sufficient cash liquidity. This prepares you for sharp market downturns and provides the flexibility to seize new opportunities.
